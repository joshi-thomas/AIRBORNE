About Our College

A Few Words
About Airborne

After the completion of primary education, the next step is to pursue professional courses with the possibility of employment. It is a dream come true to get a job. Airborne College, the number one in Kerala, is a way to raise the hopes and say hello to the unique job opportunities in front of the young generation.

Airborne was started in 2009 with the motive of enabling people from the locality to seek an education in aviation. Starting as a small institute, we make sure whoever takes admission in our institute gets affordable quality education, and we also ensure they get an opportunity to explore their own talents.

Everything you need to know about Airborne College — admissions, campus life, hostel facilities and placements.

Airborne has been shaping careers in aviation, logistics, paramedical and management since 2009. With over 15 years of experience, industry-aligned syllabi, expert faculty and strong industry connections, Airborne is one of the most trusted colleges in Kerala.

Airborne College is located in Kerala on a 2 acre campus that is easily accessible by road and public transport. Please visit our contact section for the exact address and directions.

We offer degree, diploma and IATA certification programs across Aviation, Logistics, Paramedical and Management Studies — from 6-month certificate courses to 3-year degrees and 2-year MBA programs.

Students who have completed Plus Two (10+2) in any stream can apply for our degree and diploma programs. Graduates can apply for our MBA programs, and short-term IATA courses are open to both Plus Two pass-outs and graduates.

Simply fill in the enquiry form or visit our campus. Our admission counsellors will guide you through course selection, required documents and the fee payment process.

Affordable quality education is at the heart of Airborne. Our fees are student-friendly and can be paid in convenient instalments. Scholarships are also available for deserving students.
